Ramen Protocol · Surveys of Imagined Coasts Sheet 4

Silt

Paint a mountain, then watch ten thousand years of rain carve river valleys and build small deltas where the rivers reach the sea.

Time

0years*

* A playful scale: each step of the model is counted as 1¼ years. This is not geology.

Brush

Drag on the map. Hold Alt to do the opposite. With the map focused: arrow keys move the brush, Enter paints, Space runs or halts.

To see a painted range grow gullies, paint it broad and turn the rain up: a small hill sheds too little water to cut one.

Reference

    • River, drawn wider where it drains more ground
    • Sea and lakes; waterlines follow the coast
    • Contour, every 20 m
    • Index contour, every 100 m
    • New land: built out into the sea since the survey began
    • Alluvium near the coast: over 5 m laid down since the survey began
    • Shoal: over 5 m laid down on the sea floor

    Scale true to the map on screen; the ground is illustrative.
