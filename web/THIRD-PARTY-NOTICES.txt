silt: third-party notices
=========================

silt.wasm is compiled by Zig 0.16.0 from this project's own Zig sources
(src/). When it builds for WebAssembly, the Zig compiler links in parts of
Zig's standard library and of compiler-rt, the runtime routines behind
builtins that WebAssembly has no instruction for (in silt: @exp and @sin,
used to generate the default terrain). Those two routines are ported from
musl libc. Nothing else third-party is compiled or bundled into dist/.

The page also loads a typeface from Google Fonts (listed last). It is
fetched by the browser at page load, not shipped in dist/.
